    What's included

    Built new, and built for how people actually live

    These are the things that are hard to find in Carson City rentals at any price — and they come with every home here, not as an upgrade.

    Your own garage

    A private attached garage with every home, roughly 11′ × 21′, opening directly into your entry. Park, unload, and be inside.

    Laundry in the home

    Full-size washer and dryer hookups inside every home. No shared machines, no quarters, no hauling anything down a hall.

    Gas cooking

    A gas range in every kitchen, with a dishwasher, disposal, and pantry storage standard.

    Central heat and air

    Forced-air heating and air conditioning sized for each home, on your own thermostat and your own meter.

    New, not renovated

    Built to the 2018 IBC and current Nevada energy code. New appliances, new systems, new windows — nothing inherited.

    Fire sprinklers throughout

    Every home is sprinklered and separated by a fire-rated assembly from its neighbors. Quieter, and safer.

    An accessible home

    One ground-floor home is built to accessibility standards throughout — clearances, bath, and kitchen. Ask us about it early; there's only one.

    Your own front door

    No lobby, no corridor, no shared entry. Ground homes enter at grade; upper homes have their own stair and landing.

    Vaulted upper homes

    Upper-level great rooms and kitchens are vaulted to the roofline, with windows on three sides.

    The location

    Carson City, with Tahoe over the hill

    A quiet residential street on the east side of Carson City — close enough to the capitol complex and the hospital for a short commute, far enough out that you get sagebrush and the Sierra on the skyline.
